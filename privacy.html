Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Pleshanov Publisher is a tool for managing and publishing short-form social media content. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the service.

Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Pleshanov Publisher may process:

  • basic account information provided by connected platforms;
  • content that you choose to upload, prepare, schedule, or publish;
  • publishing status, errors, and related technical metadata;
  • authentication tokens needed to connect supported social platforms.

How We Use Information

We use information only to provide the service, including connecting your accounts, preparing posts, publishing content with your permission, and improving reliability.

Platform Access

When you connect a platform such as TikTok, Pleshanov Publisher requests only the permissions needed for the selected publishing workflow. Content is published only when you authorize it.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only with connected platforms as required to provide publishing features, with service providers that help operate the product, or when required by law.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve issues, and maintain security.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can disconnect platform integrations through the connected platform settings or by contacting us. You may also request deletion of information associated with your use of Pleshanov Publisher.
